UNITED STATES SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION
ANNUAL REPORT ON FORM 10-K — Acme Corp

Item 1. Business.

We design and manufacture precision optical components for industrial automation.

Customer concentration. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Globex Corporation accounted for approximately 27% of our net sales. The loss of this customer would have a material adverse effect on our results of operations.

No other single customer accounted for more than 10% of our revenues during the periods presented. One distributor represented approximately 12% of our accounts receivable at year end.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, one customer accounted for approximately 22% of our total revenues. We do not have a long-term contract with this customer.

Suppliers. We rely on Initech, Inc. as the sole source supplier of the photonic substrate used in our flagship sensor line. An interruption at this supplier could materially delay shipments.

Regulatory matters. In March 2025, we entered into a consent decree with the Environmental Protection Agency relating to emissions at our Springfield facility.

Our top five customers in the aggregate accounted for approximately 45% of net sales; no unaffiliated customer other than those described above exceeded the disclosure threshold.
